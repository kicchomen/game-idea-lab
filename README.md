# Game Idea Lab

Small, dependency-free playable experiments. No build, CDN, account, or analytics required.

## 企画・選定方針

- 「何をずらすか」「どの一要素を編集するか」だけで発想を始めず、プレイヤーが何者になり、どんな体験や手触りを楽しむのかからも候補を出す。試作しやすさだけで一画面パズルへ収束させない。
- 題材や名前だけを変えた案を新規案として選ばない。直近の案と、主動詞・操作対象・時間構造・フィードバック・勝利条件・リトライ構造を比較し、同じ遊びの繰り返しを避ける。特に「一要素を編集→再生→結果」の反復に偏らせない。
- 選定時は、最初の5〜10秒の気持ちよさに加え、10分後の深さ、1時間後の広がり、テーマと遊びの結びつきを説明する。パズル案も、単なる面数追加に頼らず、遊びが変わる独立した展開を考える。
- アイデアを先に選び、画像を中心に全体像を紹介してから、面白さの核を小さく試遊できる形にする。試作の小ささと企画の幅を混同せず、試遊結果をPDCAへ戻す。

## Sensor Shift

Open `games/sensor-shift/index.html` on any static server. GitHub Pages path: `/game-idea-lab/games/sensor-shift/`. 

Embed: iframe width 100%, height 1040px (scrolling allowed), title `センサーずらしを試遊`. Checked iframe widths: 320px, 390px and 500px (desktop browser; no physical phone test). The board preserves its 420×480 aspect ratio. Page has a maximum width of 500px. Use a full-screen link alongside the iframe. Local trial history may be unavailable in third-party iframes; the game works without storage.

Controls: change the sensor angle (−45° to +45°, 1° increments), press 再生, observe the blue/red ray and orange turn markers, then change the angle and replay. Buttons also permit single-degree adjustment. Pause/resume/reset supported. Last 8 completed trials are stored only in this browser.

## Mechanics

The robot moves at 90 units/second. A ray points at `heading + sensor angle`. When a wall is within 100 units, the robot turns right exactly 90°. The detector re-arms after its reading exceeds 104 units. Physics runs at 120 fixed steps/second, independent of display frame rate. Touching a wall with the radius-9 body fails. Entering the exit with the robot center succeeds. A trial ends after 12 simulated seconds. There is no goal attraction or angle-specific scripted route.

The prototype implements only the distance-sensor-angle part of the original concept. Light sensors, floor sensors, sensor-position changes, stage progression, and completion scoring are intentionally outside this experiment.

## Footstep Echo / 足あとエコー

A turn-based puzzle about cooperating with the position you occupied four turns ago. Open `games/footstep-echo/index.html`. No dependencies, network calls, storage, authentication, analytics or timer. Touch-friendly directional buttons and Wait, keyboard arrows/Space, undo, reset, hints, two rooms and replay. The echo can overlap the player; only the echo presses the switch. The gate stays passable while the player occupies it, so it cannot trap the player. Blocked input is not a turn.

### Idea selection

Compared drawing a bounce surface, routing parcels, and a delayed-footstep puzzle. Chose delayed footsteps because the core can be tested with five tap buttons and visible before/after states, without reflex timing or extra resource systems.

## Tiny Tug / ちいさなタグボート

Live: https://kicchomen.github.io/game-idea-lab/games/tiny-tug/?v=2

A direct-control real-time rescue game. Approach stranded boats to attach automatically (maximum three), guide the convoy around reefs, then enter the bottom-left harbor to deliver it. Rescue all six within 90 simulated seconds. Five hull points; touching a reef damages the hull, and a trailing boat hitting a reef detaches that boat and those behind it. Detached cargo can be recovered after 1.6 seconds. Releasing input brakes. No dependencies, gameplay network requests, accounts, analytics, audio or storage.

Controls: arrows/WASD, relative drag over the sea, or held direction buttons. P/Escape pauses. Blur/hidden-page interruption pauses and clears held inputs; resuming requires an explicit action. Pause offers restart. Success/failure offers retry. Touch support is implemented; physical mobile touch remains untested.

### Idea and scope

Compared rescue skipper, market caller and creature caretaker experiences before choosing towing. This adds continuous direct steering and the growing spatial responsibility of a convoy, rather than another edit-one-parameter/replay or turn-based puzzle. `assets/tiny-tug/design-notes.md` compares the core structures and explains immediate feel, ten-minute decisions, and proposed longer-term expansion. The one-lagoon trial does not implement archipelago missions, tides, equipment or campaign progression. Concept artwork is illustrative, not a gameplay screenshot.

# Firebreak Keeper / 火の見の番人

Role: lead two crews around a fictional forest map, rescue three groups and extinguish every fire before shift change while keeping burned forest to at most eight cells. Turns advance only when pressing the wind/next-turn button. Each crew has two actions per turn. Movement, 3×3 water spray, permanent single-cell firebreaks, rescue and well refill are implemented. Three missions, full-state undo, failure, retry and next-mission flows are included. It is not real-world emergency guidance.

## Hush Courier / ねむりの谷の配達人

Static, dependency-free; no game network calls, accounts, audio, analytics or storage. The implemented core is walking/running, accumulated noise/suspicion, terrain-limited temporary hiding, a visible breathing/observation cycle, three automatic deliveries, dawn deadline, success/failure, pause and retry. It is a core demo, not a completed or human-validated game.

Stop for 0.65 seconds at a house to deliver. Hiding lasts at most 3.5 seconds; release to recover. The cycle is 4.5 seconds asleep, 1 second warning, 3 seconds observing. Blur and hidden-page interruptions pause and clear all controls; resume is explicit. Pointer release/cancel/leave/lost capture releases input.

## 旅人たちの取り引き / Roadside Bargain (2026-10-22)

A travelling mediator demonstrates fixed-value bargaining: they propose quantities, read concrete responses and revise terms until both can accept. This is a short introductory model, not adaptive AI or a deep negotiation simulation. Three conversations exchange a map for canteens, linen for bread, and oil flasks for blankets. Each item quantity is 1–4. All three meetings accept a 1↔1 exchange, and the score preview makes it possible to avoid refusals; this prototype demonstrates the offer/response/counter loop rather than deep strategy. A traveler accepts when the visible value of what they receive minus the cost of what they give is at least zero. A refusal spends that traveler’s patience; a counteroffer changes the proposed deal. After agreement, seal it or spend one patience from each traveler to seek a better split. Seal at least two of three deals to open the road. The game has multiple acceptable quantity pairs in each conversation, rather than one hidden correct allocation.

Play: https://kicchomen.github.io/game-idea-lab/games/roadside-bargain/

Controls: tap/click the −/+ quantity buttons, then propose, try a counteroffer, seal, or ask for another split. Tab and Enter/Space work with focused buttons. The static prototype has no dependencies or remote requests.
