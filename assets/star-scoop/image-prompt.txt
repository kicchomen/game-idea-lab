Use case: ui-mockup. A beautiful compact Japanese mobile browser game UI design concept, portrait composition. Title 雲間の星すくい. Navy midnight sky, warm golden stars, soft muted lavender rain clouds. Role: an amateur sky fisher in a small crescent boat scooping unknown stars through cloud. Screen top has small title and night 2/5 and bank 7/24. Center compact illustrated cloud-bag and crescent boat, last drawn golden star +2. Below show current catch 5 and cloud 1/2, remaining stars 4 clouds 2. Two large accessible buttons すくう and 帰る side by side, bottom small pause/help. Cohesive paper-cut illustration with subtle grain, high contrast large Japanese labels. Minimal screen chrome, no complex panels, no decorative tiny unreadable text. This is a concept mockup, not screenshot. Fit all controls on one phone screen.
